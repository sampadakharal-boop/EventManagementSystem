ABOUT THE EVENT

What Is The Festival About?

A Day Full of Music & Entertainment

Summer Music Festival is a vibrant celebration bringing together music lovers, talented artists, food vendors and entertainment enthusiasts for an unforgettable experience.

The festival features live bands, local artists, DJs, food stalls, entertainment activities and a headline performance in the evening.

Date July 30, 2026
Time 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Location Kathmandu
Entry Free

Event Itinerary

Here is what will happen throughout the festival.

10:00 AM

Gates Open & Registration

Attendees enter the venue, complete registration and receive event information.

11:00 AM

Opening Ceremony

Welcome speech, event introduction and opening performance.

4:00 PM

DJ Performance

A live DJ set brings the festival crowd together.

5:30 PM

8:30 PM

Closing Ceremony

Final announcements, thank you message and official closing of the festival.
